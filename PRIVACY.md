# Privacy Policy — BCS Copilot

_Last updated: 2026_

BCS Copilot ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with information.

## Information we collect
The app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do
not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

## Data stored on your device
Your practice progress, test history, and settings are stored **locally on your
device only** (using on-device storage). This data never leaves your device and
is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Purchases
If you choose to unlock premium features, the purchase is processed by Google
Play (or the Apple App Store). We do not receive or store your payment details.
Purchase validation is handled through the store's billing system and our
payments provider (RevenueCat), which may process a non-identifying purchase
token to confirm your entitlement.

## Internet access
The core app works fully offline. Network access, if present, is used only for
processing purchases through the app store.

## Children's privacy
The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data
from anyone.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
by the "Last updated" date above.
