## Full description
Prepare for the BCS Preliminary examination anywhere — no internet needed.

BCS Copilot is a clean, distraction-free practice app built around how the real
exam works: 200 marks, 200 MCQs, two hours, with negative marking.

WHAT YOU GET
• 1,800+ meticulously curated questions across all 10 subjects — every
  answer verified, zero duplicates — Bangla, English,
  Bangladesh Affairs, International Affairs, Geography, Science, ICT,
  Mathematical Reasoning, Mental Ability and Ethics.
• Full 200-question mock exam in the real BCS format — two-hour timer and
  0.5 negative marking, with the exact subject weighting of the real paper.
• Quick practice mocks (25 / 50 / 100 questions) to fit any study session.
• Topic-wise practice with clear explanations for every question.
• Performance analytics that highlight your weak areas so you know what to
  revise next.
• 100% offline — practice on the bus, in the village, anywhere.

FREE & PREMIUM
Start free with Bangla, English and Bangladesh Affairs plus 25-question
practice mocks. Unlock everything — all subjects, the full 200-question mock,
50/100 practice mocks and complete analytics — with a single one-time payment.
No subscription.

A NOTE ON CONTENT
All questions and explanations are original study material. BCS Copilot is an
independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Bangladesh Public
Service Commission (BPSC) or any government body.
